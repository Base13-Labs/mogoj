# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Mogoj welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, protect private information, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community. Report conduct concerns privately through a [GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/Base13-Labs/mogoj/security/advisories/new). Do not include another person's private information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as capacity permits, limit disclosure to people needed to resolve the concern, and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.
